About Us

Our Journey of Innovation and Growth

ACM NMIMS Indore is a student-led chapter dedicated to fostering technical excellence, innovation, and professional growth. Over the years, the chapter has evolved into a vibrant community where students collaborate on projects, participate in workshops, engage with industry experts, and contribute to meaningful technological initiatives.

Through events, competitions, research opportunities, and leadership experiences, ACM continues to empower students to learn, build, and lead in an ever-evolving digital world.
