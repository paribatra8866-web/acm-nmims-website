About The Event

Bridging Concepts with Practical Defense

CyberForge moves beyond standard lectures, immersing students into real-world threat mechanisms, security analysis, and guided defensive implementation.

A comprehensive program built to transform theoretical awareness into actionable cybersecurity expertise.

CyberForge is a two-day cybersecurity learning and hands-on experience designed to move students from understanding cybersecurity concepts to applying them in a practical setting. The event combines expert interaction, an in-depth workshop, live demonstrations, and hands-on participant activities.

  • In-depth technical workshop guided by industry cybersecurity experts
  • Live exploit demonstrations and defense architecture breakdowns
  • Hands-on practical challenge with dedicated technical mentorship
  • Official participant certificate distribution from ACM-W NMIMS Indore
