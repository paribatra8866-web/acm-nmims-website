ACM NMIMS Indore

Founded to foster technical excellence and innovation, ACM NMIMS Indore has grown into a vibrant community of developers, researchers, designers, and technology enthusiasts.

Over the past two years, the chapter has hosted workshops, hackathons, speaker sessions, and collaborative projects that have impacted hundreds of students.
